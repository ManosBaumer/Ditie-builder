About

Ditie is a metro-builder sandbox over real demand across Guangdong, Hong Kong, and Macau.

Maps

Each prefecture is its own map, clipped to its administrative border. The Metro tab highlights cities that already run a metro or urban rail system.

Demand

Living and job bubbles show where people live and work. Places mark non-commute draw: stations, campuses, hospitals, malls, parks, and attractions.

Accounts

Accounts and cloud saves use Supabase Auth and Postgres. Local saves still work when you are signed out.
